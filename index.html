Siflo — Whistle signals

Whistle signals and messages for your group, from phone to phone, even without a network. For canyoning, climbing, caving and every outing where the whistle does not always get through.

Support

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to contact@aidiligence.eu.

Free, for good

Siflo is free, with no ads and no account. If it helps your outings, you can leave a tip from the app's settings, through Google Play.

Siflo adds to your whistle and radio; it does not replace them. Radio waves do not go through rock.
